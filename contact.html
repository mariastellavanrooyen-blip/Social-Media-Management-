Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our social media management services.

How quickly will I see results?

Most clients see meaningful engagement growth within 4–6 weeks. Significant follower growth and lead generation typically builds over 3–6 months as the algorithm recognises your consistent, quality content.

Do you manage both Facebook and Instagram?

Yes — we specialise in both platforms. Our Bloom and Flourish packages include full management of Facebook and Instagram, tailored to each platform's unique algorithm and audience behaviour.

What niches do you work with?

We work across all niches — wellness, retail, hospitality, fitness, trades, real estate, fashion, coaching, food & beverage, and e-commerce. Every content strategy is niche-specific.

Do I need to provide content or photos?

Not necessarily. We can work with content you provide, source styled stock imagery that matches your brand, or advise you on simple photography you can capture yourself. We make it easy.

Will I have input into the content?

Always. Every month you'll receive a content calendar for approval before anything is published. Your brand voice and messaging is always the priority.

Is there a minimum contract period?

We recommend a 3-month minimum for meaningful results, but we offer month-to-month flexibility. Social media is a long-term investment — results compound with consistency.
