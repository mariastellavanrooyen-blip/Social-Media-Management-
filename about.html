About Maria Social Media Co.

Small, local, and deeply invested in your brand's growth — one post at a time.

Our Story

Born From a Passion for Connection.

Maria Social Media Co. was founded with one belief: every small business deserves a powerful social media presence — not just the ones with big marketing budgets. We started as a local social media management service and have grown into a trusted partner for businesses across every niche.

We combine deep knowledge of the Facebook and Instagram algorithms with genuine creativity and a commitment to your brand story. Every piece of content we create is intentional, strategic, and designed to convert.

We stay ahead of every algorithm update, trend shift, and platform change — so you can stay focused on running your business.
